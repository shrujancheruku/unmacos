Make your own

A theme is one colors.toml in ~/.local/share/unmacos/themes/<name>/, in Omarchy's format, so Omarchy themes drop straight in. Copy one, change the colours, then pick it from the menu or run unmacos-theme-render <name>.

A theme can also ship its own app config, such as a hand-tuned ghostty.conf, to replace the generated one. Wallpapers go in ~/.config/unmacos/backgrounds/<theme>/.

Tokyo Night is UnMacOS's own hand-tuned palette. The rest come from Omarchy (MIT), and each downloads Omarchy's wallpapers on first use.
